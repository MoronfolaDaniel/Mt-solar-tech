ENERGY INSIGHTS

Planning your solar and storage system

Start with what you need to power, then assess how the equipment will work together.

Understand your energy use

List the appliances or equipment you need to run, their rated power and typical operating hours. Identify essential loads and equipment with high starting demand.

Set your backup priorities

Consider which loads must remain powered and for how long. Battery capacity, usable energy and inverter output all affect system suitability.

Assess the site

Available roof space, shading, electrical supply and installation conditions affect the design. A qualified assessment helps identify practical constraints.

Check compatibility

Panels, inverters and batteries need to work together. Confirm specifications and warranty terms for the actual models being supplied.

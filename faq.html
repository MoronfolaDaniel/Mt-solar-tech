Frequently asked questions

Useful information for your next energy decision.

Which products can I enquire about?

Solar panels, hybrid inverters, lithium batteries, residential and commercial storage, all-in-one ESS, solar street lights and EV charging systems.

How do I order?

Choose a product and use Order Now to prepare an order enquiry. Our team must confirm specifications, availability and pricing before an order is agreed. This website does not accept payment.

How do I choose a system?

Share your appliances or equipment, usage hours, available installation space and backup requirements. System sizing and installation requirements need assessment.

Does the cart place an order?

No. It stores your selection in this browser so you can prepare a product enquiry. It does not reserve stock or process payment.

How do I request support?

Use the contact form with the Support enquiry type. Include the product model and a description of the issue; do not open electrical equipment yourself.
